## 6. გარემოს ცვლადებთან დაკავშირება (.env Integration)

```env
# BRAND & BUSINESS
BRAND_NAME="TechStore Georgia"
BUSINESS_TYPE="Electronics & Gadgets Retailer"
SUPPORTED_LANGUAGES="ka,en"
CONTACT_INFO="+995 32 2 100 200, info@techstore.ge"
WORKING_HOURS_AND_TIMEZONE="Mon-Sun 10:00-20:00 (Asia/Tbilisi)"
HUMAN_HANDOFF_POLICY="Escalate on complaint, dispute, payment mismatch, or user request"

# BANKING
TBC_IBAN="GE00TB0000000000000000"
BOG_IBAN="GE00BG0000000000000000"
RECIPIENT_NAME="TechStore Georgia LLC"
PAYMENT_DESCRIPTION="Order {orderId} Payment"

# DELIVERY & SHIPPING
CITY_DELIVERY_FEE=5.00
REGIONAL_DELIVERY_FEE=9.00
EXPRESS_DELIVERY_FEE=10.00
FREE_SHIPPING_THRESHOLD=100.00
```
